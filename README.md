## Assessment Problems

*   Problem 1: Exploring Petal Length. 

    This part investigates the distribution of petal lengths in the Iris dataset through statistical analysis and visualization. By plotting histograms and overlaying normal distribution curves, the analysis aims to understand the patterns and variability in petal length across all 150 flowers and compare the three iris species.  
    The goal is to explore the differences between species and evaluate whether a normal distribution provides a suitable representation of petal length data.
